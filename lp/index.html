06Security & access

One gate
into the keep.

The relay holds every decision your organization makes, so it is built as the trust boundary.

  1. i

    Checked at the door.

    Every connection joins with a session that must have write access to your repository. Identity comes from the session — never from what a client claims.

  2. ii

    Yours to decide.

    Only a card’s recipient can decide, delete or undo it.

  3. iii

    Your accounts. Your say.

    Gmail, Slack and Notion are authorized per person. Nobody connects them on your behalf.

  4. iv

    Leave anytime.

    Delete your account from inside the app, whenever you like.
